Berklee Teachers on Teaching 2027

A gathering rooted in Berklee's community—educators who come together to share, reflect, and grow as teachers, artists, and human beings. Through collaboration and dialogue, we explore new ideas, challenge assumptions, and inspire the student and artist within each of us.

Excellence in Teaching & Learning Cultivating professional excellence and developing innovative fields and practices to foster an equitable and inclusive culture of teaching and learning.
Community & Care Respecting the rights, dignity, and creative expression of all, while supporting the well-being of colleagues and students.
Integrity & Authenticity Embracing honesty, courage, and openness as guiding principles in the work of teaching and learning.
Creativity & Curiosity Committing to continuous growth and improvement by learning from the past to imagine the future, fostering empathy and cultural awareness through the arts.

Each year, BTOT provides space for Berklee's academic community to connect across disciplines—examining pedagogy, performance, technology, belonging, wellness, and the evolving landscape of arts education. By gathering in this way, we strengthen our ability to prepare students to realize their creative and career potential in a rapidly changing world.

Spring 2026 Survey Results

What BTOT Means to Faculty

In April we surveyed Berklee faculty on what the conference means to them, and what they want BTOT 2027 to be.

Peer-to-Peer Learning
Faculty view BTOT not as a place to be lectured at, but as a rare window into the minds of their peers — what they teach, how they teach, and why they teach. Terms like "collective knowledge," "fellowship," and "witnessing our resources" reflect that the best content comes from faculty-to-faculty exchange.
"A Balm Against Burnout" & Catalyst for Inspiration
For many, the conference serves an emotional purpose as much as an educational one — an "annual dose of inspiration" that genuinely shifts how faculty interact with students and inspires real changes in classroom dynamics.
